How to install

  1. Download Encore.ipa above on your computer.
  2. Install Sideloadly (free, Windows or Mac).
  3. Plug in your iPhone, drag Encore.ipa into Sideloadly, sign in with your Apple ID and press Start.
  4. On the iPhone: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management, trust your Apple ID.
  5. Open Encore and enter your invite code.

Encore is invite-only: ask the person who shared this page for a code. With a free Apple ID, reinstall every 7 days (your music stays).
