How to install

  1. Download Encore.ipa on your computer.
  2. Install Sideloadly (free, Windows or Mac).
  3. Plug in your iPhone, drag the file into Sideloadly, sign in with your Apple ID, press Start.
  4. On the iPhone: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, trust your Apple ID.
  5. Open Encore and enter your invite code.

Encore is invite only. With a free Apple ID, reinstall every 7 days. Your music stays, and Encore reminds you.
